# The Listening Station

The 2026-09-09 additions use the existing plum-black, bone-cream, gold, cyan,
and violet palette. Their shared subject is ordinary astronomy infrastructure
under an immense sky: quiet, weathered, and hopeful.

## Wallpapers

`backgrounds/05_raiders_listening_field.jpg`,
`06_raiders_penumbra.jpg`, and `07_raiders_afterlight.jpg` were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. No
reference images, screenshots, promotional artwork, or game assets were
supplied to the generator. The prompts describe generic telescopes,
observatories, terrain, and astronomical phenomena, and exclude franchise
characters, machinery, landmarks, branding, and insignia.

The tool returned 1672x941 PNGs. The delivered JPEGs are 3840x2160, exported
with ImageMagick's Lanczos filter, a centred crop to exact 16:9, quality 95,
and 4:4:4 chroma sampling. These are upscaled 4K exports, not native 4K
generations. The poster-style wallpapers alongside them are unchanged, and
the two nebula variants that used to sit between them were dropped.

The exact prompts and export settings are recorded in
[`wallpaper-prompts.json`](wallpaper-prompts.json).
